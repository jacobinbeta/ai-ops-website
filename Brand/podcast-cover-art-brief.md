# Cover Art Brief: Rolling Forecast Podcast

Please design square cover art for my podcast, **Rolling Forecast**. Everything you
need is below. Start by giving me 3 different design directions, then I'll pick one
to refine.

---

## About the podcast

- **Show name:** Rolling Forecast
- **Tagline:** Fractional CFO founders on how they built, grew and are reinventing with AI.
- **Host:** Jacob Krogue, CPA
- **Produced by:** AI Operations Partners (a company that helps Fractional CFO firms find new clients and use AI)
- **What it's about:** Candid interviews with founders of Fractional CFO and accounting
  firms about how they started, how they grew, and how AI is changing the way their
  firms work. Every guest answers the same core questions, then the conversation goes
  off-script.
- **Audience:** Fractional CFOs, accounting firm owners, and finance professionals
  thinking about starting their own firm. They're smart, busy business owners who
  respect numbers and plain talk.
- **Platforms:** Apple Podcasts, Spotify and YouTube

## The name

A "rolling forecast" is a finance term for a forecast that keeps getting updated as
new information comes in, instead of being set once a year. It fits the show: an
ongoing, always-updating look at what's working for Fractional CFO firms. Visual ideas
that play on this are welcome, for example a trend line moving forward, a line chart
that keeps extending, or forward motion. Keep it simple and avoid clip-art (no piggy
banks, dollar signs, stacks of coins or generic stock-chart graphics).

## Mood

- Professional and trustworthy, but modern and approachable, not stuffy
- Clean, confident, minimal
- Should feel like a smart business show, not a personal finance or investing show

## Brand colors (from my website)

| Color | Hex | How it's used |
|---|---|---|
| Deep teal (main brand color) | `#00595d` | Primary color, use it the most |
| Charcoal | `#343a40` | Text and dark accents |
| Light teal tint | `#edf4f4` | Soft backgrounds |
| White | `#ffffff` | Text on dark backgrounds, space |

A small bright accent (for example a warm coral or amber) is fine if it helps the
design stand out, but teal should clearly be the main color.

## Fonts (from my website)

- **Headings:** Manrope (bold, geometric sans-serif). Use this style for "Rolling Forecast".
- **Body:** Inter (clean sans-serif). Use this style for any smaller text.

If you can't use these exact fonts, use a similar bold, modern sans-serif.

## Logo

My company logo is a simple, solid teal triangle shaped like a letter "A" with a small
cutout. You don't need to include it, but if you do, keep it small and subtle in a corner.

## Text on the cover

- **Required:** "Rolling Forecast" (large and easy to read)
- **Optional, small:** "with Jacob Krogue, CPA" or "A podcast for Fractional CFO founders"
- Keep the text short. Two lines at most besides the title.

## Must-follow rules

- **Square, 3000 x 3000 pixels** (Apple's recommended size), RGB colors, saved as PNG or JPG
- **Must be readable as a tiny thumbnail.** Most people will see it at about the size
  of a postage stamp on their phone, so the title has to be big and high-contrast.
- **Don't include** Apple, Spotify or YouTube logos, website addresses, or words like
  "new" or "episode 1". Podcast apps can reject covers that include these.
- No small detailed text or busy backgrounds
- Should look distinct next to other business podcasts. Many use a photo of the host on
  a plain background, so a strong graphic design will stand out.
