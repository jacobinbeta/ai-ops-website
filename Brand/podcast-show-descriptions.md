## Tips for getting more listeners

- **Keep the show name as is.** Don't stuff extra keywords into the title on Apple or
  Spotify; Apple can reject shows for that. The description is the right place for them.
- **Ask for ratings early.** Reviews in the first few weeks help the most with Apple's
  and Spotify's charts. Mention it at the end of your first few episodes.
- **Launch with 3 episodes at once.** New listeners are more likely to follow a show
  that already has a few episodes to try.
- **Use searchable episode titles.** Lead with the topic, then the guest, for example
  "How to Price a Fractional CFO Offer | with Jane Smith".
- **Give guests clips.** Short video clips that guests post on LinkedIn are one of the
  best ways to reach new Fractional CFO listeners.
