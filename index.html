The problem

Referrals built your practice. They don't make growth predictable.

Referral clients are often your best clients. But you don't control when they arrive, and that makes it hard to plan capacity, hiring, or revenue.

Feast or famine

A strong quarter, then a quiet one. The next engagement depends on someone else remembering to mention you.

Selling is a second job

Done properly, outbound means list building, writing, follow-up, and scheduling every week. For most owners, those hours come straight out of billable work.

Stop-start outreach doesn't work

Outreach gets pushed aside the moment client work picks up. Without consistent follow-up, good conversations go cold.

FAQ

Common questions

What does "fully managed" mean?

We handle strategy, technology, copywriting, sending, reply handling, and ongoing optimization. You approve the targeting and messaging at the start. After that, your main job is taking the calls that land on your calendar.

How does the AI SDR work?

The AI SDR is an AI assistant trained on your services, your ideal client, and how you describe your practice. When a prospect replies, it responds promptly, answers common questions, checks fit against criteria you set, and offers times from your calendar. Anything outside its rules goes to a person.

Will prospects know they are talking to AI?

The AI SDR never claims to be a person. If a prospect asks, it says plainly that it is an AI assistant working with your practice, and it can bring a person into the conversation at any time.

Who decides what "qualified" means?

You do. We agree the criteria at kickoff, such as company size, industry, role, and timing. Only prospects who meet them are offered time on your calendar.

How long until I see results?

Setup takes 2 to 3 weeks before outreach goes live. How many calls get booked depends on your market, your offer, and the targeting, so we set realistic expectations together on the discovery call. We do not promise specific numbers.

What does it cost, and is there a long contract?

There is a one-time setup fee and a flat monthly management fee, scoped to your target market and outreach volume. After a 90-day initial term, it is month to month. We share exact numbers on the discovery call, once we know what your practice needs.
